# Manual scenarios

Scenarios verified by hand at handover instead of by an automated test. Each row is that scenario's one claim, and the traceability check reads this table ([docs/conventions/testing.md § Every scenario has exactly one test](../docs/conventions/testing.md#every-scenario-has-exactly-one-test-the-traceability-check)). The file doubles as the handover checklist.

| account-check | A real payment brings the notification on the iPhone | Only a real Revolut notification fires the owner's Shortcut, and only Apple's push service and the lock screen show the result, and how long it took; the tests stop at the fake push service ([ADR-0008](../docs/adr/0008-phone-triggered-check.md), [ADR-0010](../docs/adr/0010-web-push.md)). | account-balance | The grey balance moves in step with the waking-up bar | jsdom runs no animation and lays nothing out, so only a real phone shows the band crossing the grey balance and the bar under "Server se probouzí" moving as one, both on the one waiting clock (`--duration-wait`, pinned to the document timeline's zero, c019 design.md D9); the tests only see that the balance is grey and moving. It needs a sleeping server: the first opening after a quiet spell of more than half an hour, the app signed in and keeping a balance, opened from the Home Screen icon. From the moment the bar appears (1.5 s), the band and the bar must start and end each crossing together, with no visible lag between them (at most a frame, c019 design.md § Risks). On an older iOS without `background-clip: text`, the grey shows without a band: that fails the row, and is noted with the iOS version. First chance: right after c019's merge deploys, on the owner's iPhone; again at M7 on the daughter's. | M7 | |
| account-balance | Under reduced motion the grey balance stands still | `prefers-reduced-motion` acts in CSS, which jsdom does not apply, so only a real phone shows the grey balance standing still (c019 design.md D9: no animation, no band, and no fade when it turns ink). Reduce Motion is turned off again afterwards. First chance: right after c019's merge deploys, on the owner's iPhone; again at M7 on the daughter's. | M7 | |
